Backups are pg_dump archives encrypted with age (public key {{ public_key }}). A restore replaces all data, including users and feeds, with the contents of the backup. If the backup is older than your account you will be signed out and must sign in with the credentials that existed then.

Keep a copy somewhere safe and separate from this host: without it, backups cannot be decrypted (age -d -i liquidator-backup.key file.dump.age > file.dump).
